STORMROYALE

Build. Battle. Survive the storm.

Best on iPhone — add to Home Screen for full screen.

How to play

  • Left stick — move
  • Right stick — aim & auto-fire
  • 🧱 Build — enter build mode, tap a piece, tap right stick to place
  • ⛏️ Harvest — hold to swing your pickaxe at trees, rocks & walls for mats
  • 🔄 Reload — reload your weapon
  • Open chests & grab floor loot for guns, ammo and shields
  • Stay inside the white circle — the storm hurts!
  • Be the last one standing for a Victory Royale 👑
